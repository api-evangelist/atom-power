The short answer An RPP, or Remote Power Panel, is a distribution panel that sits between upstream switchgear and the loads it serves, taking one large power feed and splitting it into individual branch circuits. It's called "remote" because of where it sits, physically away from the main electrical room, not because it does anything wireless.
